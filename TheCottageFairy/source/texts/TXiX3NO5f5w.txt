Rural Life in December
2020-12-10

the winter fairies have kissed my valley sending spirals of frost over water and field the lakes reflect a frozen world and the river froth is icy cold the willows and birch slumber the cottontails hide away yet i am awake witness to stillness cast in crystal

as the year end fast approaches i'm gathering materials to add some festive cheer to my home now that my young willow tree is dormant i'm taking a handful of shoots knowing that its abundant growth will supply me with more next year

evergreens are plentiful in washington state and a vital resource to local animals their needles carry an amazing scent that makes my head spin with thoughts of wintertide every december i love to bring the cozy smell into my home and look forward to gathering a small bundle with long and cold winter months ahead i seek out these little projects that will bring some nature's beauty and color into my home so

do if like many of us this year you are spending the holidays in small groups or by yourself i encourage you to show some love to your home in simple ways making it a quaint little nest that exudes all the lovely feelings you seek to cultivate do

so do if you feel alone or anxious treat this as a time of self-discovery an opportunity

to reach out to others in creative ways in my case i prefer to purchase less and give handmade gifts instead a favorite is to share home baked goods or at least the ones i don't eat since i am saving a lot of my dried fruits for winter i decided to get creative and add some more unusual ingredients to my cookie dough i must say it turned out quite splendid so

i have not felt lonely for a long time but years ago the feeling was a constant companion to all who may be feeling that way i encourage you to take a deep breath and remember that nothing stays the same and there will be a tomorrow there is so much light and love in this world it is everywhere waiting to be enjoyed by those who choose to see it you will be okay and you will learn so much about who you are and what you need in your life i hope you spend this week reading your

favorite books and watching your favorite movies as well as finding creative ways to get back to your community into nature in these unusual times thank you so much for your support i appreciate your beautiful comments and all your amazing words of wisdom sending you my love have a lovely day or night
