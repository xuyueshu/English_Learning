Poetry and Wildcrafting - Rural Living vlog
2020-09-02

let me take you to a place nestled among the mountain slopes where glaciers weep the spring away and the birds sing you can find me where the fairies are disguised in a splendor of silver wings they flit then dive among the huckleberries and make love in the grass

watch how the boughs nod heavy with fruit

the sweet tang melts into my daydreams though the summer is nearly at an end my

lips are stained in spirit light anticipating the sunset of the season and the dawning of the next have you ever been called an old soul maybe wild child i have and it made me feel as if i was unusual or that maybe i didn't quite fit into the ideals of a modern world but i came to see that the opposite is true that there are many old souls thriving right now sharing their love of nature history and craftsmanship with the world

they are not out of place but actively inspiring a future where we value the earth as it should be and embrace simple living over conspicuous consumption you can be an old soul that thrives in a modern world simply by choosing hope over despair attuning yourself to nature and embodying what inspires you it all begins and ends with you you are enough that is where you can make the greatest impact and connection that is all you need to do

do so

do no you
