I got married! - a little countryside farm wedding
2022-05-23

My wedding was on a passing May day. The morning began the way I love most: quietly and patiently waiting for the twilight to give way to the sun. And then I went to choose the flowers I would use for my wedding bouquet. I have such nervous excitement,

you know, like butterflies flying in my stomach. So, I'm really looking forward to starting the ceremony, because the anticipation of so many events is a little scary, but in a good way. This is difficult to explain. I made my own bouquet because I knew it was very important to me . These are comfrey, arnica, and balsam root. These are flowers that have always brought me a lot of joy and lifted my spirits when I was outdoors. So it was fitting to thank them by including them in this special day. It looks like I

painted my nails terribly, but we'll ignore that and move on. Now I'm trying on a dress, taking some photos among the wildflowers, and then I'll meet my family. I knew it was important for me to spend the morning in peace and reflection, preparing for the meeting to begin the holiday. I send you my love. I'm worried, happy, worried and happy again. So, I hope you have a great week. See you soon. Goodbye. The day turned out to be beautiful.

There were light rains and we saw a rainbow. Even during the ceremony, a small hummingbird flew to us. It was just wonderful. And I am so happy that I can share with you at least a small part of our holiday.
