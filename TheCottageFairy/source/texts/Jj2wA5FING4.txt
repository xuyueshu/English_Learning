This year, we fully enjoyed the blessings of each season, from the warm summer harvest to the cool autumn days . However, this winter was a bit tough. Compared to previous years, the amount of snow in the valley was extremely low, and the snow that did fall melted quickly, leaving the entire hillside barren and dry . It's certainly not an ideal winter; the ground is muddy and slippery. Nevertheless, I try to keep myself busy while waiting for spring to arrive . Last week, my husband had to leave the house for a short while , and I was alone for the first time in a long time . The dog spent the day with its parents' new puppies, so it enjoyed spending time with the cats and rabbits

. I've become so used to living with people around me that spending time relaxing without being disturbed by anyone felt strangely nostalgic . I had a very fulfilling time, and it made me realize once again how important it is to have time to myself from time to time in life . We are inherently connected to the world around us, but at the same time, we also have the potential to build a rich inner life , which can save us from many difficulties . One day I will have to say goodbye to my pets and loved ones , but despite these inevitable events, I know that the only way I can survive is to connect with my own soul .
