Most techniques were reserved for men, so watercolor was considered one of those "feminine " art forms that women could pursue. And so of course, that was something that Beatrix Potter knew, she became a true master of watercolor, and that really inspired me to get into watercolor as well . This woman created such a whimsical world with her art that it inspired me to fall in love with watercolor painting as well.
