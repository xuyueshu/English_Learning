the peace of small moments - cottage life
2020-08-20

today i felt the need to cultivate peace in the best way i know how by creating alongside nature and practicing slowness on an earth that grows lavender and golden glows where blue mountains rise above the clouds i think that peace of mind is not only achievable but grows in abundance once we begin to nourish it

when some flower beds needed to be cut back i decided to make a flower crown something about it stirs in my heart the memory of a little girl running through the woods with dandelions stuck in her hair i find nothing creates a sense of calm more than connecting with childhood memories that you cherish remembering that there was a time when the world is full of nothing but hope and possibilities and then realizing that things can still be that way my prayer for peace in mind i went to

the labyrinth beside my house and took the walk of life a labyrinth isn't a maze though it may seem to be full of confusing twists and turns however the path will lead you to your destination as you walk you will realize that the purpose of the labyrinth isn't to confuse you but to offer a meditation on life a new perspective challenging you to see beyond the next two steps and recognize the beauty in the big picture it is a dry hot windy day

and i was having quite a hard time staying cool so i kept myself busy by recording a little song for you guys it's one of my favorite fiddle tunes i'm not a professional fiddler but i am very excited to share this song even though i myself was aware of the mistakes i made in the recording i knew i had to take a step back and just accept that all i wanted to do was share something that means a lot to me and doing my best at it and i have to be at peace with whatever my best is i don't actually play fiddle that much it's something that i pick up now and then when i feel the need to be more grounded or connect with nature

since i like to go outside and play to the skies and offer up my music in a place where i can be alone yet sometimes i feel like i do have an audience and that nature is appreciating my my mediocre efforts
