making lilac tea is a treat that I look forward to all year i watch with anticipation waiting for when the blooms are just ready for harvesting we have an abundance of herbs and flowers available to us that can be made into delicious tea you can usually grow your own herbs and flowers from seed but you can also purchase them at your local nursery

separating the sweet blossoms from the more bitter stems is a time-consuming process I often think of it as a sort of labor a meditation in teaching me how to be patient and thoughtful and in the present after the tea is brewed for a few minutes I usually add something to give the cup a little bit of sweetness the first sip of tea is always the most

satisfying I love the taste and smell it is always worth the effort to put love and patience into everything you do the end result is that much better of course there's nothing like a freshly brewed cup of tea to inspire your creativity I always find myself painting with a cup next to my hand I'll see you guys next time I upload videos every
