2022-05-29

A few days have passed since the wedding. We're both back at work, so I've been busy, but I'm trying to take short moments to enjoy the last breath of spring. Summer is coming soon, and I've been truly enjoying this cooler season. Thanks to him, the flowers here bloomed much longer than usual. Right now, all over the valley, so

many flowers are preparing to reproduce, and so many trees are shedding their seeds. This is truly magical. When the wind blows, it looks like snowfall. And I just lay on the ground outside, watching it fly across the sky. The other day I rediscovered the secret garden—that's what my mother-in-law called this little corner of lilac bushes, which really seems like a very cozy place of

magic. With all the lilacs in bloom, it's the perfect time to gather some flowers for a very special project. So, the day has finally come to put my old ice cream maker to the test. And I'm really excited because I picked

lilac flowers, and my goal is to make lilac ice cream, because I've already made lilac syrup, and it was incredibly delicious. She is growing so fast . If you look at the video from just a few weeks ago, she fit so easily into the frame, and now she's just huge. So yeah, the wedding was about a week ago, and the few days leading up to it were really interesting. When we arrived at the venue a few days before, it was such a beautiful sunny day. Yes, everyone agreed that it would most likely be a beautiful day. But that morning I got up, and almost immediately it started to rain. At first I wasn't too

worried because it was just a light rain , but then it started to pour harder, harder and harder. We have everything prepared for an outdoor ceremony . So we were a little worried, because there were a lot of elderly people among the guests, and I'm sure there were a lot of others who wouldn't want their beautiful holiday clothes to get completely soaked in the pouring rain . Usually, in such situations, I am quite optimistic. I always think that even the unexpected adds spice to the story and makes the experience even better. But it was a little

worrying here because I knew that people didn't have a change of clothes, and the rain was very heavy. It was a real downpour. Several people in my family noticed that I was very worried about this situation. And it was very kind of them to remind me: in Hispanic culture, this is often considered good luck. If it rains, it means you are receiving blessings from heaven, and that is such a touching thought. But then it was time for the ceremony, the clouds parted, the sun came out, and it became so clear and beautiful. Those beautiful sun rays were breaking through the clouds, and since it was suddenly sunny, all the insects, small

wild animals, and other creatures became very active. We had a ceremony, and even during it, a cute little hummingbird flew in, stopped right in front of us, just to say hello. These are always very curious birds. It meant a lot to me. I've seen hummingbirds at some pretty important moments in my life, and they've always been special birds to me, especially lately, because there are so many of them this spring at a time when I'm going through so many emotions, transitioning into a new stage of life. It was very magical because the sun was shining right during the ceremony, and later, when the rain stopped and evening came, a

huge rainbow appeared in the sky. I think we often harbor fantasies in our minds about how we would like a certain day or part of our life to be, believing that everything should be just this way and no other way, and spontaneity or surprises are not welcome at this moment . And I know that for some people, especially those who have n't always felt in control of their own lives, events, or what's going on around them, it can be very difficult to just let go of the situation and accept the flow of the moment and whatever

fate throws at them. If it were a perfectly beautiful sunny day with no problems, it would of course still be a great day. But thanks to all these little mishaps and surprises that were resolved, the day became so magical and beautiful precisely because it wasn't perfect, and I just wanted to share that with you. Accept your days as they come, even if there are moments you are unsure about. So, I think I've been talking long enough. So now I'm going to try making ice cream . result will be some really delicious ice cream that I can eat. So, I'm cooking. This ice cream was, to put it mildly an experiment. I
