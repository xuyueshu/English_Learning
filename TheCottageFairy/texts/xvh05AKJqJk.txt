children's stories fables and folklore often feature anthropomorphized animals these stories hold much value to me because my love for animals has been an innate part of my person since i was born in times of great sadness in my life i found that having to care for an animal much like caring for a child would force me to step outside of myself and focus on another living being to serve instead of dwell on my melancholy

so wild animals impact me in a different way if you've ever had the honor of looking straight into the eyes of a creature of the wilderness be it the perfectly round oculus of an owl or the soft brown oval of a deer moose or bear it is an experience that stays ingrained in your memory it is both magical and humbling once i found myself returning the gaze of a great horned owl while carrying firewood back to my mother's home we regarded each other for a long while

its dark eyes shocked me with a reminder that this elegant creature knew so many things that i could never even begin to understand it was a complex biology built from countless ancestors that had come before it had so many skills that i would never be able to attain a formidable challenge to our human perception of what it is to have higher intelligence i encourage you to seek out a natural area near your home and see what animals you encounter no matter how commonplace or small each one has a fascinating story to tell
