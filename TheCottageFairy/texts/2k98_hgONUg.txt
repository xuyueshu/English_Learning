You hardly saw me at home this spring. Despite being 9 months pregnant, even though I felt very heavy and tired, I just couldn't resist the call of the wildflowers. I sat on the hillside for hours, writing in my journal, distracting myself from the pain in my body. I wrote down poems. I drew fairies and gnomes. I described my thoughts and some fears in detail. Pouring it all out on paper. In the end, I closed the book, hid my pen, and walked home the long way through the lupine fields. Allowing things to be as they are. It gets easier every time I've

been mostly offline for the last few months as I've been recovering and learning to be a parent. I watched some of my early videos on this channel, which are many years old now, and I felt a bittersweet taste, watching a young woman in her 20s, still so unsure of what to do with her life, and not knowing how to heal her body and soul. My heart always responds to her. She stumbled for years, often falling back into old habits of imbalance and poor health. But over time, she began to realize that she was tired of dragging herself along for so many years. It's hard to leave behind what's familiar, even if it hurts you . But now everything is different. This forced surrender to the moment actually became a gift, a requirement to simply exist in the present. The buzz in my head is drowned out by a burning desire to focus on what I can do moment by moment. I think we can all apply this a little more in our lives.
